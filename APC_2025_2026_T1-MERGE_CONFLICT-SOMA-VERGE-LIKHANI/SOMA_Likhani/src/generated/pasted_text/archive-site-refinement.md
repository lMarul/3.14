1. Apply WCAG Typography Standards to Terms of Use and Submission Guidelines Pages

The text on the Terms of Use and Submission Guidelines pages currently appears too small compared to the rest of the site.

Minimum typography rules

• Body text minimum: 16px
• Secondary labels / supporting text minimum: 14px
• Buttons minimum: 14–15px bold
• Small uppercase labels minimum: 13–14px with proper letter spacing

No important content should appear below accessible reading size.

The goal is for these pages to remain readable for all users without feeling dense or visually tiring.
